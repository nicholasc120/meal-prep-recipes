---
title: Steak
---

## Ingredients

- Steak
- Salt, Pepper
- Olive oil
- Butter
- Aromatics (garlic, rosemary) 

## Instructions

1. Pat steak dry on both sides with a paper towel
2. Salt both sides of the steak and then put in the fridge for at least 30 minutes. 
3. Season with pepper or rub
4. Heat a stainless steel pan on medium heat and add oil
5. Once heated, place steak into pan away from you. Let sit for 2 minutes
6. Flip steak and cook for 1 minute
7. Turn off the heat, remove the steak, and let the pan cool to a low heat.
8. Add 2tbsp butter and aromatics. Baste the steak for about 30 seconds on each side
