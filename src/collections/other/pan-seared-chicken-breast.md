---
title: Pan Seared Chicken Breast
---

## Ingredients

- Chicken Breast
- Seasonings (salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika)
- Olive oil
- Butter

## Instructions

1. tenderize and flatten the chicken breast
2. season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika
3. Heat a stainless steel pan on medium heat and add oil
4. Once heated, place chicken into pan away from you. Cook on each side for about 3 to 4 minutes
5. add knob of butter and baste. Remove from pan when internal temp is about 160 and let rest
