---
title: Sheet Pan Roasted Vegetables
---

## Instructions

1. Chop whatever vegetables you have into similar-sized pieces.
2. Toss with olive oil (1 to 2 tablespoons of olive oil per pound of vegetables, or roughly 1 to 2 tablespoons per baking sheet) and seasonings
3. Roast at 400°F for 20–25 minutes, flipping halfway.

you can also add 2:3 balsamic glaze:olive oil and 1:3 honey to olive oil
