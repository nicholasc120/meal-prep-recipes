# STREET CORN PASTA 🌽

https://www.facebook.com/reel/945961207830826

## Macros 
Per serving (makes 12)
545 Calories
45g Protein

## Ingredients

### STREET CORN
- 900g (32oz) frozen corn
- 180g (1.5 cups) Cotija
- 120g (1/2 cup) FAGE 0% Greek yogurt
- 75g (1/3 cup) light sour cream
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- Handful of cilantro, chopped
- Juice & zest of 2 limes
- 1 Tbsp chili powder
- 1 Tbsp Tajín
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder

### CHICKEN
- 1150g (40oz) chicken breast, pounded thin
- 15g (1 Tbsp) oil
- Salt, black pepper, garlic powder & chili powder (add enough of each to coat chicken)

### PASTA
- 680g (24oz) ditalini
- 900g (32oz) chicken bone broth
- 354g (12oz can) fat free evaporated milk
- Garlic powder & salt, to taste

### FINISHING
- 180g (3/4 cup) FAGE 0% Greek yogurt
- 80g (2/3 cup) Cotija, finely grated
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Tajín, to taste
- Pinch of salt
-
1. Spread frozen corn on a sheet pan and broil 3-5 minutes until lightly charred. Mix with remaining street corn ingredients, cover & refrigerate.
2. Coat chicken with oil, add seasonings, and add to the same sheet pan that you cooked the corn on. Bake at 375°F for about 30 minutes, then finely chop.
3. Bring bone broth to a boil. Add ditalini and evaporated milk, reduce to medium-low and simmer ~20 minutes, stirring frequently, until pasta is cooked and thickened. It is more important to stir frequently in the final 10 minutes to prevent sticking
4. Stir in chicken, Greek yogurt, Tajín & cilantro. Remove from heat, fold in street corn and Cotija, then rest a few minutes before serving.
