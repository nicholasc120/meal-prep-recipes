# Chicken Teriyaki

https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/#recipe

4 servings - 488 Cal, 45gP

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken
- 3 Chicken Breasts (900 Cal, 168gP) (butterfly these and score horizontally)
- 1/4 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1.5 Tbsp neutral oil (180 Cal)

### For the Teriyaki Sauce
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce (27 Cal)
- 3 Tbsp mirin (60 Cal)
- 3 Tbsp sake (substitute with Xiaoxing Wine (40 Cal) or water)
- 1.5 Tbsp sugar (72 Cal)

### Rice
- 200g of rice (716 Cal, 13gP)
