# Yakiniku Don

https://www.thespruceeats.com/yakiniku-sauce-for-grilled-meat-2030920

cook this on a nonstick pan

##  6 portions - 481 Cal, 44gP

## Ingredients
### Beef
- 1.138kg Thinly Sliced Strip Loin (1600 Cal, 250gP)  OR 3 pound top sirloin into ⅛-inch-thick slices (1350 Cal, 234gP)
### Rice
- 270g Uncooked Rice (900 cal, 18gP)
### Sauce (~10 tbsp)
- 2 clove garlic
- 1/2 cup soy sauce (68 Cal)
- 4 tbsp mirin (80 Cal)
- 5 tsp granulated sugar (81 Cal)
- 4 tsp sesame oil (160 Cal)
- 3 teaspoons ground roasted white sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon whole roasted white sesame seeds
