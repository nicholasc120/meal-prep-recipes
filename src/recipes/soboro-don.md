# Tori Soboro Don

## Macros
3 servings (569 Cal, 42gP)

## INGREDIENTS:
- Ground chicken: 454g (635 Cal, 91gP)
- Eggs: 4 (320 Cal, 26gP)
[Seasoning]
- White wine: 8 tbsp (96 Cal)
- Sugar: 3 tbsp (146 Cal)
- Soy sauce: 3 tbsp (30 Cal)
- Grated ginger: 2 tsp
[Rice]
- 135g Uncooked Rice (480 Cal, 9gP)

## Instructions
1. Sauté ground chicken over medium-low heat, breaking it up until it changes colour, and wipe off any excess moisture.
2. Add seasonings and simmer, stirring constantly, until the liquid reduces. (Note: Leave a bit of sauce in the pan for the eggs)
3. In the same pan, make scrambled eggs. 
4. Serve them on top of a bowl of rice.

## KEY POINTS:
- In this recipe, we're swapping traditional Sake and Mirin for white wine and sugar in a certain conversion formula. As a Japanese home cook, I assure you it works perfectly to replicate the authentic taste. Only one simple rule you need to follow: :point_right: Make sure to use a DRY and LOW-ACIDITY WINE (e.g. Pinot Gris). A cheap one is fine, but avoid using fruity wine
