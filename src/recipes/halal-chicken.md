## Macros
6 servings (576 Cal, 42-44gP)

- this is more rice than i usually use and you could cut it down if Calorie count is too high

## Ingredients

### Chicken: 
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into ½–¾-inch bite-sized pieces (1200 Cal, 224gP)
- 1 Tbsp + 1 tsp (13g) kosher salt
- ½ tsp (~1g) ground turmeric
- 2 tsp (7g) ground cumin
- 1 tsp (3g) ground coriander
- ½ tsp (1g) freshly cracked black pepper 
- 2 tsp (7g) smoked paprika
- ½ tsp (1g) cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp (1g) dried oregano
- 3 Tbsp (45mL) fresh lemon juice
- ¼ cup (60g) harissa paste (100 Cal)
- Vegetable oil, for searing
- 1 clove garlic, grated

### Rice:
- 400g long-grain basmati rice (1422 Cal, 26gP)
- 1 tsp (3g) ground turmeric
- ½ tsp (1g) ground cumin
- ¼ tsp (~1g) onion powder 
- ½ tsp (~1g) garlic powder
- 1 tsp (3g) MSG, or chicken bouillon powder 
- 1½ tsp (5g) kosher salt
- 2 cups chicken stock, or water (30 Cal, 2gP)
- 2 Tbsp (28g) unsalted butter, cold and cubed (201 Cal)

### White Sauce:
- ¾ cup (180g) light mayonnaise (420 Cal)
- ½ cup (120g) greek yogurt (60 Cal, 12gP)
- ½ tsp (2g) granulated sugar (8 Cal)
- ½ tsp (~1g) MSG
- Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste 
- 1½ Tbsp (23mL) fresh lemon juice
- 2 tsp (10mL) white distilled vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, grated

## Instructions

### Chicken:

In a large bowl, combine the chicken thighs with the kosher salt, turmeric, cumin, coriander, black pepper, smoked paprika, cayenne, oregano, lemon juice, and harissa paste. Mix thoroughly until the chicken is evenly coated. Cover and refrigerate overnight or up to 2 days for best flavor.
Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium-high heat and add enough vegetable oil to lightly coat the surface.

Working in batches if necessary, add the marinated chicken in a single layer. Sear for 2–3 minutes until well browned, then flip. Reduce the heat to medium and continue cooking until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C).
Transfer the cooked chicken to a sheet tray or cutting board and let rest for a few minutes. Immediately toss the hot chicken with the grated garlic to lightly perfume the chicken.

### Rice:
Add the rinsed basmati rice to the base of a rice cooker along with the turmeric, cumin, onion powder, garlic powder, MSG or bouillon powder, kosher salt, and chicken stock or water. Stir to combine and start the rice cooker according to the manufacturer’s instructions 
Fluff the rice with a fork, then add the cold cubed butter and gently mix until melted and fully incorporated. Keep warm until ready to serve.

### White Sauce:
In a medium bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, greek yogurt, sugar, MSG, lemon juice, vinegar, and grated garlic until smooth.
Season to taste with salt and pepper. Cover and refrigerate until ready to use.
