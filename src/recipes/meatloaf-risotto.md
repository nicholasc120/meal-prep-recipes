# Meatloaf
https://natashaskitchen.com/meatloaf-recipe/

6 servings - 580 Cal, 42gP

## Ingredients (1889 Cal, 193gP)
- 2 lbs ground bison, 85% or 80% lean (1452 Cal, 180gP)
- 1 med onion, (1 cup), finely chopped
- 1 tsp olive oil (40 Cal)
- 2 large eggs (160 Cal, 13gP)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 Tbsp ketchup (38 Cal)
- 3 Tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 3/4 cup Panko breadcrumbs, or gluten-free bread crumbs (165 Cal)
- 1/3 cup milk (34 Cal)
- 1 tsp salt, or to taste
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper

## Meatloaf Sauce Ingredients (265 Cal):
- 3/4 cup ketchup (175 Cal)
- 1 ½ tsp white vinegar
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar (90 Cal)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder

can add 90g of Parmesan for a total of 2544 Cal, 229gP total, or 508 Cal, 46gP for 5 servings
